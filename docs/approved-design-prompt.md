GAME IDENTITY: An original Roblox creature collection and evolution game with two primary pillars: fast-paced 1v1 real-time arena combat and elemental Rift Walking exploration. Combat is the primary attraction; Rift Walking is the main method of acquiring and evolving fighters. Every creature and evolution is obtainable through gameplay, without a paywall.

COMBAT: Accessible Dragon Ball-like 3D arena combat, but with entirely original characters and mechanics. Tekken-like two-fighter tracking CAMERA ONLY, not Tekken gameplay. The camera frames both fighters, zooms with separation, tracks aerial movement, and avoids sudden flips. Controller and keyboard/mouse first. Global design emphasis approximately 70% ground combat and 30% aerial, with different character specializations. Fast directional movement, forward/back/side dash, dodge, guard, jump, limited air dash, light and heavy attacks, two specials, ultimate, attack states, cancel windows, hitstun, blockstun, combo scaling, counters, launchers, and anti-infinite protections. Simple inputs, deep discoverable combo routes. Training mode with dummy, damage and input displays.

PVP: Casual 1v1 best-of-three, invited friend duels best-of-three, ranked 1v1 best-of-five. Ranked unlocks through modest early-game milestones without mandatory wins. Skill, creature power, and connection-aware matchmaking. Separate turn-based 3v3 mode later. Secure server-controlled match results, anti-exploit validation, friend invites, accept/decline, timeout, privacy, disconnects and rematches.

RIFT WALKING: Solo and optional two-player co-op. All content remains obtainable solo. Standard expedition target 6–8 minutes. Modular handcrafted room layouts, randomized encounter choices, exploration, combat, Rift event, guardian and extraction. Expedition, Hunt and Trial missions. CO-OP: Maximum two players, optional party invitations and ready checks, individual loot, revives, combo assists, elemental PvE synergy, rejoin and disconnect recovery. Initial duo scaling approximately 170% enemy HP and 135% enemy count, provisional. No co-op-only creature or evolution.
